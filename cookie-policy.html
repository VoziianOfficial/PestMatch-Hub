What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help improve functionality and user experience.

How We Use Cookies

PestMatch Hub uses cookies to support basic website functionality, remember user preferences, and improve performance.

Types of Cookies

  • Essential cookies: required for the site to function
  • Performance cookies: help analyze usage
  • Preference cookies: remember user settings

Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some features of the site may not function properly if cookies are disabled.
