Introduction

PestMatch Hub is an independent platform designed to help homeowners and property users connect with local service providers. PestMatch Hub does not perform pest control services directly and is not a contractor.

By accessing or using this website, you acknowledge that information may be collected and processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • ZIP code
  • Property type
  • Service category selections
  • Request details or message content you submit through forms

We may also collect certain technical or usage information, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • General interaction data on the site
  • Cookie or preference-related data

How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and improving the website
  • Processing and routing quote or service requests
  • Connecting users with independent local providers
  • Communicating with users regarding submitted requests
  • Understanding usage trends and improving site performance
  • Maintaining site security and preventing misuse
  • Supporting internal recordkeeping and compliance needs

Sharing Information

PestMatch Hub may share submitted request information with independent service providers, partners, vendors, or service-related parties for the purpose of helping route, evaluate, or respond to a user inquiry.

Information may also be shared with technology vendors, analytics tools, hosting providers, communication tools, or professional advisors where reasonably necessary to operate the website or business processes.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, regulatory request, or to protect rights, property, operations, or safety.
